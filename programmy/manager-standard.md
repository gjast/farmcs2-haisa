# \[Manager Standard] Account Manager & Activity Booster

#### **Manager Standard** (Standard Activity Booster & Account Manager) isn't just a panel, it's a full-fledged account manager with an activity booster. It's exactly what I keep my main farm on — around **1000 accounts** — and sell the drop from. I leave SIH for small stuff and manual cases, but I run the whole volume here.

<h2 align="center">🛒 <mark style="color:green;">Why it's needed and how it differs from SIH</mark></h2>

* #### **Scale.** Thousands of accounts in one interface. SIH is built around a single inventory in the browser, while here it's the whole farm at once.
* #### **Mass selling of the drop.** I list the drop from all accounts at once, without going into each inventory separately.
* #### **Account management.** Statuses, groups, bulk actions — you can see who's alive, who's banned, who has what on their balance.
* #### **Activity Booster.** Boosting activity and hours so the accounts look alive instead of a pile of bots.

<h2 align="center">🖥️ <mark style="color:green;">System requirements</mark></h2>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Manager Standard has its own hardware and system requirements — noticeably stricter than "just another panel". The developer tests the software on a specific configuration, and it's best not to stray from it.
{% endhint %}

* #### **Windows 11 Pro x64**, latest version. It also works on Win10, but the developer verifies specifically on 11 — get it to avoid catching extra bugs.
* #### **Pagefile by the formula:** RAM + pagefile **≥ 120 GB**. That's more than in the PC section — for Manager Standard, plan for headroom ahead of time.
* #### **A discrete GPU.** If you farm on a discrete GPU — disable the integrated one via BIOS, otherwise you can catch game crashes. If you're only setting up the Activity Booster, you can skip this step.

<h2 align="center">💡 <mark style="color:green;">How I use it</mark></h2>

1. #### I keep the main farm — about **1000 accounts** — entirely in Manager Standard.
2. #### The drop collects on the accounts, and I list it in bulk from all of them at once, not one inventory at a time.
3. #### I track statuses: alive, banned, with a balance — and work with them in groups.
4. #### The Activity Booster spins activity so the accounts don't look dead.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Why not SIH at scale.** SIH works great when you have one or two collectors and open the inventory by hand. But on a thousand accounts, going into each one isn't work, it's punishment. Manager Standard closes exactly this pain: bulk operations across the whole farm at once.
{% endhint %}
