<h2 align="center">🍀 <mark style="color:green;">How earning on Armory works</mark></h2>

#### The Armory Pass is an in-game pass. While you play, stars accumulate on it, and you later spend them in the in-game shop on an item from the available collections. One pass gives **40 stars**, and you can hold **5 passes** at once — that's **200 stars** per account.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Where's the catch?**

One pass costs about **$15**. To level all five at once, you need to put roughly **$75** into an account. So the entry threshold here is several times higher than with cases, and the cost of a mistake is accordingly higher too.
{% endhint %}

<h2 align="center">💵 <mark style="color:green;">How to earn instead of gifting money away</mark></h2>

#### The key difference between Armory and cases: here you choose yourself what to turn your stars into. And most collections **mathematically put you in the red**. Opening everything indiscriminately is a guaranteed way to burn your budget.

**It's convenient to check the stats on** [**CS Roi**](https://csroi.com/armory) — in the armory tab you can see what percentage of your investment comes back on average for each collection.

### <mark style="color:blue;">What ROI means in plain terms</mark>

#### **ROI (Return on Investment)** is the percentage that on average comes back from what you put in. If a collection has 150% ROI, then for every hypothetical $100 you get $150 back on average. "On average" is the key word: over short distances it's all luck, over long ones it's statistics.

{% hint style="warning" %}
And the most important thing people forget to account for: the **13% Steam fee**. You have to subtract it from ROI. A collection with 110% ROI after the fee is already almost zero — and factoring in time and risk, it's a loss.
{% endhint %}

### <mark style="color:blue;">The practical takeaway</mark>

#### Only open collections that give a clear profit after subtracting the fee. Everything else is gambling, not business. And double-check the numbers before **every** round: ROI shifts with the market, and what was profitable two weeks ago can be a trap today.



{% hint style="success" %}
As of summer 2026, the CS2 economy has gotten a lot worse. It's tied to very rare updates and other things — even when 2 new armory collections came out, they had very low ROI. In practice, Armory farming is of no interest to anyone right now; at most you can keep stars for a "rainy day" for when a new armory case might come out that you could earn on, nothing more.
{% endhint %}
